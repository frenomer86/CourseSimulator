# Bioinformatics: degree-structure research

Research notes for the CS Virtual Lab programme `bioinformatics`. University names appear only in these notes. Sources were read in October 2026; where a page could not be retrieved directly (certificate errors, 403) the figure is marked "via search summary" and should be re-checked before it is quoted anywhere public.

## 1. Summary

- Bioinformatics is taught in three shapes. In the US and Canada it is a bachelor's major built on a biology and chemistry foundation plus computing (UC San Diego, Florida State, Calgary). In the UK and China it is a 3 or 4 year BSc with a research project in the last year (Liverpool/XJTLU, Zhejiang-Edinburgh). In South Africa, Canada and the UK the main route is postgraduate: a one-year Honours or course-based master's (Pretoria, Stellenbosch, Guelph, Manchester, Johns Hopkins) followed by a thesis master's and a PhD.
- Bachelor's degrees run 4 years and consist of roughly 40 courses: calculus, discrete maths or statistics, programming and data structures, chemistry/biochemistry, genetics and molecular biology, then sequence analysis, databases, genomics and an advanced lab or thesis.
- Master's degrees run 1 to 2 years, 10 to 12 courses or 36 to 48 credits plus a project. The common core is: molecular biology or genomics, algorithms for bioinformatics, statistics for bioinformatics, machine learning, programming/tools.
- PhD programmes are research degrees of 4 to 5 years: 4 to 8 courses in the first two years, lab rotations, a qualifying examination (written proposal plus oral) by the end of year 2, a dissertation proposal or candidacy examination by year 3, teaching, and a dissertation with publications.
- The platform has 13 bioinformatics courses at bachelor level only. The gaps are: chemistry/biochemistry and genetics foundations, systems/network biology, a capstone, and the whole master's and PhD layers (algorithms, statistics, advanced genomics, machine learning, pipelines, research methods, seminar, proposal writing).

## 5. Common core across universities

Courses that appear at two or more of the universities above:

1. Calculus and linear algebra; discrete maths or probability and statistics (UC San Diego, Florida State, Calgary, Zhejiang).
2. Introductory programming, then data structures and algorithms (all bachelor programmes; Johns Hopkins lists them as master's prerequisites).
3. General and organic chemistry, biochemistry, cell biology (UC San Diego, Florida State, Calgary, Liverpool, Johns Hopkins prerequisites).
4. Genetics and molecular biology (every bachelor programme and the Johns Hopkins and Academia Sinica master's/PhD cores).
5. Sequence analysis and alignment, biological databases (UC San Diego, Calgary, Pretoria, Stellenbosch).
6. Statistics for biology and statistical methods in bioinformatics (Florida State, UC San Diego PhD core, Guelph, Johns Hopkins, Academia Sinica).
7. Algorithms for bioinformatics or advanced algorithms (Johns Hopkins, UC San Diego PhD, Stellenbosch, Academia Sinica).
8. Genomics, next-generation sequencing, transcriptomics (every master's programme).
9. Machine learning for biological data (Johns Hopkins core, Stellenbosch, UC San Diego option, Liverpool AI).
10. Phylogenetics, structural bioinformatics, systems and network biology (electives or final-year units).
11. A research project, thesis or advanced lab (every level).
12. Seminar, ethics and research communication at PhD level (UC San Diego, Academia Sinica, Pitt colloquium).

## 6. Prerequisite chains

- Programming: introductory programming (Python or C) then data structures then algorithms then algorithms for bioinformatics (MIT lists algorithms and probability as prerequisites; Johns Hopkins requires programming and data structures before admission).
- Mathematics: calculus then linear algebra and probability then statistics for biology then statistical methods in bioinformatics and machine learning (MIT prerequisites: algorithms, probabilistic systems, fundamentals of biology).
- Biology: biology and chemistry then organic chemistry/biochemistry then molecular biology and genetics then genomics, sequence analysis and structural work (UC San Diego BIMM 143 prerequisites: two introductory biology courses or recombinant DNA techniques).
- Bioinformatics: molecular sequence analysis then biological databases then advanced bioinformatics lab (UC San Diego numbering: 181 before 182 before the lab).
- Master's: genomic methods and software tools before the master's project (Guelph); two foundational courses before the dissertation.
- PhD: coursework in years 1 and 2, then qualifying examination, then candidacy examination, then dissertation.

### 7.2 Master's core: MIT 6.047 / 6.878 Computational Biology (cross-listed undergraduate and graduate, 2 lectures per week plus recitation)

Prerequisites: algorithms, probabilistic systems analysis, fundamentals of biology. Grading includes an NIH-style term-project proposal and a journal-style paper. Fall 2015 lecture list:

1. Introduction: biology, algorithms and machine learning.
2. Sequence alignment I: dynamic programming, global and local.
3. Alignment II: database search, BLAST, BLOSUM.
4. Hidden Markov models I: Viterbi and forward algorithms.
5. Hidden Markov models II: posterior decoding, Baum-Welch.
6. Transcript structure: gene finding, RNA-seq mapping, assembly, differential expression.
7. Expression analysis: clustering and classification.
8. Networks I: Bayesian inference, deep learning, network dynamics.
9. Networks II: network learning, spectral methods.
10. Regulatory motifs: discovery, Gibbs sampling, EM.
11. Epigenomics: ChIP-seq, peak calling, chromatin states.
12. RNA modification, editing, splicing regulation.
13. Human ancestry and population history.
14. Disease association mapping and GWAS.
15. Quantitative trait mapping and eQTLs.
16. Missing heritability and interpreting GWAS.
17. Comparative genomics and evolutionary signatures.
18. Phylogenetics: tree building and inference.
19. Phylogenomics: gene and species trees.
20. Personal genomics and disease epigenomics.
21. 3D chromatin interactions (Hi-C).
22. Genome engineering with CRISPR/Cas9; project presentations.

A second master's anchor is the Manchester Programming Skills unit (15 credits, semester 1): CoCalc online environment, Python fundamentals with biological data (syntax, loops, files, functions, regular expressions, modules), Unix command line (pipes, redirection), then building standalone pipeline programs in a project group; 12 hours of lectures, 24 of practicals, three projects weighted 20, 30 and 50 percent.

### 7.3 Doctoral seminar format

Public week-by-week lists for doctoral seminars were not found; the formats are:

- Pittsburgh biomedical informatics journal club (1 credit, weekly): students present and critique recent papers and each other's presentations; and the colloquium (1 credit, weekly guest speakers, graded on participation).
- UC San Diego: seminar in bioinformatics and systems biology plus student research talks, every quarter of years 1 and 2.
- Academia Sinica: seminar for six semesters and four student presentations within three years.
- Suggested weekly pattern for the platform seminar: week 1 how to read a paper; weeks 2 to 6 student-led paper discussions on open problems; weeks 7 to 9 reproduce a published result (scenario engine with evidence and rubric); weeks 10 to 12 qualifying-style mock proposal and mock panel; week 13 to 14 writing a paper section; final week presentations.

Current programme (13 courses): molecular-biology-for-computing, introduction-to-python, probability-and-statistics, biostatistics, data-structures-and-algorithms, database-systems-and-design, sequence-analysis-and-alignment, genomics-and-next-generation-sequencing, phylogenetics-and-molecular-evolution, structural-bioinformatics, data-science, machine-learning, legal-and-ethical-issues-in-computing.

| Gap | Evidence | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Programme is only 13 courses; peers have 26 to 40 | All bachelor programmes above | Add maths and CS foundations from existing slugs |
| No calculus, linear algebra, discrete maths in the programme | UC San Diego, Florida State, Calgary, Zhejiang | Reuse calculus, linear-algebra-for-data-science, discrete-mathematics |
| No chemistry/biochemistry or genetics | All bachelor programmes | New: Biochemistry and Cell Biology for Computing; Genetics and Population Genomics |
| No systems/network biology | Liverpool, Zhejiang, UC San Diego, Johns Hopkins | New: Systems Biology and Biological Networks |
| No capstone | UC San Diego, Calgary, Liverpool, Zhejiang | New: Computing Capstone Project (shared with other programmes) |
| No master's or PhD layer | Johns Hopkins, Guelph, Stellenbosch, UC San Diego | New master's courses below; reuse deep-learning, big-data-and-parallel-computing, cloud-computing, NLP and responsible-ai |
| No seminar or proposal-writing course for doctoral training | UC San Diego, Guelph, Academia Sinica | New: Doctoral Research Seminar in Bioinformatics; Research Proposal and Grant Writing |

Engine fit: E6 (alignment, HMM and tree visualiser), E1 (Python/R code runner), E5 (database and SQL), E7 (maths lab for statistics, Monte Carlo, SymPy), E10 (scenario cases for ethics and research design), E4/E12 (Linux terminal and cloud console for pipelines) cover the practical content. Wet-laboratory techniques, microscopy and instrument operation have no engine and are taught as data-interpretation cases in E10.

## 9. Proposed structure

### Bachelor of Science in Bioinformatics (4 years, 25 core plus 12 elective pool, 6 electives required)

Core in suggested study order:
fundamentals-of-computer-science, principles-of-programming, introduction-to-python, calculus, discrete-mathematics, linear-algebra-for-data-science, probability-and-statistics, **Biochemistry and Cell Biology for Computing (new)**, molecular-biology-for-computing, data-structures-and-algorithms, database-systems-and-design, **Genetics and Population Genomics (new)**, biostatistics, sequence-analysis-and-alignment, statistical-inference, data-wrangling-and-preparation, genomics-and-next-generation-sequencing, phylogenetics-and-molecular-evolution, structural-bioinformatics, machine-learning, data-science, **Systems Biology and Biological Networks (new)**, research-methods-in-computer-science, legal-and-ethical-issues-in-computing, **Computing Capstone Project (new, shared)**.

Electives: data-visualisation, deep-learning, artificial-intelligence, big-data-and-parallel-computing, epidemiology-and-public-health-data, numerical-methods-and-computation, programming-with-c, programming-with-cpp, version-control-and-devops, theory-of-computation, optimisation-for-machine-learning, operating-systems.

### Master of Science in Bioinformatics (1 to 2 years, 6 core plus 9 elective pool, 4 electives required)

Core: Advanced Algorithms (new, shared), Advanced Statistical Modelling (new, shared), Advanced Computational Genomics (new), Advanced Machine Learning (new, shared), Scientific Workflows and Reproducible Data Pipelines (new), Graduate Research Methods (new, shared).
Electives: Probabilistic Graphical Models (new, shared), deep-learning, structural-bioinformatics, phylogenetics-and-molecular-evolution, big-data-and-parallel-computing, cloud-computing, natural-language-processing, epidemiology-and-public-health-data, responsible-ai.
Milestones: project proposal, mid-programme seminar, research project or two-semester thesis, oral defence, responsible-conduct training.

### Doctor of Philosophy in Bioinformatics (4 to 5 years, 5 core plus 5 elective pool, 3 electives required)

Core: Doctoral Research Seminar in Bioinformatics (new), Research Proposal and Grant Writing (new, shared), Advanced Algorithms, Advanced Statistical Modelling, Advanced Computational Genomics (the last three are waived or replaced by electives when already taken at master's level).
Electives: Probabilistic Graphical Models, Advanced Machine Learning, deep-learning, structural-bioinformatics, Scientific Workflows and Reproducible Data Pipelines.
Milestones: lab rotations (year 1); qualifying examination (written literature review and proposal plus oral, by end of year 2); teaching practicum; ethics training; candidacy/dissertation proposal defence (by end of year 3); at least one first-author paper; annual committee reports; dissertation and defence.

New courses: 4 bachelor, 7 master's, 2 PhD (the limits). Details are in `proposals/bioinformatics.json` in the session notes folder.

## 10. Doubtful points

- UC San Diego bachelor requirements and the Georgia Tech credit hours come from search summaries because the pages gave certificate errors; the master's/PhD pages on the UC San Diego catalogue and the JHU, Guelph and Academia Sinica documents were read directly.
- Stellenbosch and Pretoria figures come from search summaries (the pages returned 403 or redirects); Manchester's full unit list was not retrieved. No bachelor's bioinformatics major was found in Africa, and no Cape Town, Wits or Makerere programme page could be retrieved. Treat African entries as Honours-route evidence only.
- No public week-by-week list for a doctoral bioinformatics seminar was found; section 7.3 gives formats, not topics.
- Web search was capped part-way through the research, so Nairobi, Ghana and other Asian universities (NUS, Tsinghua, KAIST) were not reached; Asia is represented by Academia Sinica/National Taiwan, XJTLU and Zhejiang.
