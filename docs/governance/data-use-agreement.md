# Data use agreement (template)

Between the CS Virtual Lab project ("the Provider") and the researcher(s) named in the
approved data access request ("the Recipient"). Governed by the laws of Ghana, including the
Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. **Dataset.** The Provider supplies the release identified in the request: de-identified
   engagement events of learners who consented to research use, in the formats and date range
   stated, with its codebook and the version of the privacy notice the participants saw.
2. **Purpose.** The Recipient uses the dataset only for the study described in the request.
   Any other use needs a new request.
3. **No re-identification.** The Recipient will not attempt to identify any participant, to
   link the dataset with other data about individuals, or to contact participants.
4. **No onward sharing.** Only the named people in the request may access the data. The
   Recipient will not pass the dataset, or any subset at the individual level, to anyone else.
5. **Security.** The data is kept encrypted at rest, on access-controlled systems, and never on
   shared or public storage. Any suspected loss or unauthorised access is reported to the
   Provider within 72 hours.
6. **Outputs.** Published results contain aggregates only. No table, figure or example may
   describe fewer than five participants. The Recipient sends the Provider a short summary of
   findings and a copy of each publication.
7. **Citation.** Publications cite the dataset by its release id (and DOI when one exists) and
   acknowledge the CS Virtual Lab project and the learners who took part.
8. **Retention and destruction.** The Recipient destroys all copies by the date in the request
   and confirms this in writing.
9. **Participant rights.** If a participant withdraws consent, the Provider stops future
   releases of their data. Already-delivered releases are not recalled, as the privacy notice
   states, because they do not identify the participant.
10. **Breach of this agreement** ends access, requires immediate destruction of the data, and
    may be reported to the Recipient's institution and ethics committee.
